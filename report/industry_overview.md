# Semiconductor Industry Overview

## What are Semiconductors?

Semiconductors are materials used to manufacture integrated circuits (ICs) that power modern electronic devices. They are fundamental to applications including smartphones, computers, automobiles, industrial automation, telecommunications, artificial intelligence (AI), and defense systems.

## Global Industry

The semiconductor industry forms the backbone of the global digital economy. Manufacturing is highly specialized and geographically concentrated, with a small number of companies leading advanced fabrication technologies.

The semiconductor value chain consists of:

- Chip Design
- Electronic Design Automation (EDA)
- Wafer Fabrication (Foundries)
- Assembly, Testing, Marking and Packaging (ATMP/OSAT)
- Distribution and End Applications

## India's Position

India has established a strong position in semiconductor design services but has limited domestic wafer fabrication capability. Recent government initiatives, including the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM), aim to strengthen manufacturing through financial incentives, infrastructure development, and international partnerships.
