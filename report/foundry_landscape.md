# Global Foundry Landscape

The global semiconductor foundry industry is highly concentrated, with a few companies accounting for the majority of worldwide manufacturing capacity. Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) is the dominant player, followed by Samsung Electronics and Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation (SMIC).

| Company | Market Share (%) |
|---------|-----------------:|
| TSMC | 67.1 |
| Samsung | 8.1 |
| SMIC | 5.5 |
| UMC | 4.7 |
| GlobalFoundries | 4.6 |
| Others | 10.0 |

## Key Insights

- TSMC accounts for more than two-thirds of the global pure-play foundry market.
- The foundry industry has high barriers to entry due to capital requirements, process technology, and ecosystem maturity.
- India's semiconductor manufacturing ambitions will require long-term investment and strategic partnerships to compete in this industry.

**Source:** TrendForce, Global Foundry Market Share (2024).
