# Executive Summary

India has emerged as one of the world's largest semiconductor consumers but remains highly dependent on imports for advanced chip manufacturing.

Recent initiatives such as the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM), Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme, and investments from Tata Electronics, Micron, and CG Power present an opportunity to strengthen domestic manufacturing.

This project evaluates India's semiconductor ecosystem using market analysis, competitive benchmarking, strategic frameworks, and publicly available industry data to identify practical pathways for developing a globally competitive semiconductor manufacturing ecosystem by 2030.

The analysis focuses on identifying the most attractive investment priorities across fabrication, assembly and testing, semiconductor design, and advanced packaging.
