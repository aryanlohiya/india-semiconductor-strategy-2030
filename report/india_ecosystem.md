# India's Semiconductor Ecosystem (2026)

## Executive Snapshot

India has significantly accelerated its semiconductor ambitions through the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM). As of 2026, the country has transitioned from announcing semiconductor projects to commissioning commercial manufacturing facilities while simultaneously developing its first commercial wafer fabrication plant.

---

# India Semiconductor Mission (ISM)

The India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) was launched in 2021 with an outlay of ₹76,000 crore to establish a comprehensive semiconductor manufacturing ecosystem.

The mission focuses on:

- Semiconductor fabrication (Fabs)
- Assembly, Testing, Marking and Packaging (ATMP)
- Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test (OSAT)
- Semiconductor design
- Supply chain development
- Semiconductor talent development

# India's Strengths

- Strong semiconductor design ecosystem employing thousands of engineers.
- Government-backed incentive programmes supporting semiconductor manufacturing.
- Rapidly expanding electronics manufacturing industry.
- Large pool of engineering graduates.
- Growing domestic demand for semiconductors driven by automotive, consumer electronics, AI, telecommunications and industrial applications.

---

# Key Challenges

- Commercial wafer fabrication ecosystem is still under development.
- Heavy dependence on imported semiconductor manufacturing equipment.
- Limited domestic ecosystem for semiconductor-grade chemicals, gases and materials.
- Requirement for continued investment in advanced manufacturing talent.
- Strong competition from established semiconductor manufacturing hubs.

---

# Strategic Outlook

India's semiconductor strategy currently focuses on building manufacturing capabilities across the complete value chain rather than immediately competing in leading-edge semiconductor technology. The near-term priority remains establishing reliable fabrication, packaging, testing, and supply-chain capabilities while strengthening domestic semiconductor design and engineering talent.

---

## Key Insight

India has moved beyond policy announcements and entered the execution phase of semiconductor manufacturing. With multiple commercial ATMP/OSAT facilities operational and the country's first semiconductor fabrication plant under construction, the next phase of growth will depend on successful project execution, ecosystem development, and supply-chain localization.
