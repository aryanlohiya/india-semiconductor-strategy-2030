# Problem Statement

India has announced ambitious plans to become a major semiconductor manufacturing hub through the India Semiconductor Mission and Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme. However, the global semiconductor industry is highly concentrated among a few countries and companies with decades of manufacturing expertise.

This project aims to evaluate India's current position in the global semiconductor value chain and identify the most viable strategic pathway for achieving global competitiveness by 2030.

## Research Question

How can India develop a globally competitive semiconductor manufacturing ecosystem by 2030?

## Objectives

- Analyze the global semiconductor market.
- Benchmark leading semiconductor manufacturers.
- Evaluate India's strengths and limitations.
- Assess opportunities across fabrication, ATMP, packaging, and chip design.
- Recommend strategic priorities supported by market and industry analysis.
